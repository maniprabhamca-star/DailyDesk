Worth deciding before I build

Three open questions

1. Does the grid actually beat filenames?

That's the whole bet. State 2 is the test — if you find yourself reading names rather than looking at pictures, the previews aren't earning their weight and the honest move is to stop after step 3 of the build.

2. Is triage in scope for v1, or does it ship as a viewer?

Deleting is the reason people open a folder, but it's also the part that only works in two browsers and carries real risk. A viewer-only v1 ships sooner and is defensible on its own.

3. How deep do we go into subfolders?

The local tool has a Subfolders switch. On the web, someone picks a folder with node_modules in it and we try to preview 40,000 files. I'd cap depth and show the count before rendering — but the ceiling is a real decision, not a detail.
